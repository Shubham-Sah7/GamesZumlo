## 🏗️ Layout Structure (Matching Reference)

### Top Section - Brick Formation
**Pattern**: Diamond/pyramid shape (9 rows)
```
      🧱🧱🧱           Row 1: 3 bricks
    🧱🧱🧱🧱🧱         Row 2: 5 bricks
  🧱🧱🧱🧱🧱🧱🧱       Row 3: 7 bricks
🧱🧱🧱🧱🧱🧱🧱🧱🧱     Row 4-6: 9 bricks each

    🧱🧱🧱🧱🧱         Row 8: 5 bricks
      🧱🧱🧱           Row 9: 3 bricks
```

**Total Bricks**: ~57 bricks (structured pattern)
**Spacing**: 6px padding between bricks
**Size**: 50px × 24px per brick
**Top Offset**: 40px from top

### Middle Section - Gameplay Area
**Purpose**: Large open space for ball movement
**Height**: ~60% of screen
**Features**:
- Plenty of room for rebounds
- Clear ball visibility
- Landing prediction shadow
- Motion trail

### Bottom Section - Paddle Zone
**Paddle Position**: 50px from bottom (very close)
**Paddle Size**: 160px × 16px (prominent)
**Features**:
- Always visible
- Clearly draggable
- 3D depth effect
- Deep teal color

## 🎮 Key Improvements

### 1. **Paddle - Most Prominent** ✅
**Before**: 140px × 20px, 80px from bottom
**Now**: 160px × 16px, 50px from bottom

**Features**:
- Positioned at very bottom (like reference)
- Wider for comfortable gameplay
- Deep teal color (stands out)
- 3D depth with shadows
- Smooth easing movement
- **Instantly recognizable**: "This is what I control"

### 2. **Ball - Highly Visible** ✅
**Size**: 12px radius (larger)
**Color**: Orange (#FFA366) - high contrast
**Features**:
- Soft outer glow
- Motion trail (6 frames)
- 3D gradient
- White highlight
- **Landing prediction shadow** (subtle ellipse)

### 3. **Bricks - Monument Valley Style** ✅
**Size**: 50px × 24px (smaller, more numerous)
**Pattern**: Diamond/pyramid (structured)
**Features**:
- 3D depth (3 layers)
- Soft shadows
- Gradient surfaces
- Rounded corners (6px)
- Premium mobile game quality

### 4. **Landing Prediction** ✅ NEW!
**Feature**: Subtle shadow showing where ball will land
**Implementation**:
- Ellipse at paddle level
- Only shows when ball moving down
- Fades based on distance
- Orange tint (15% opacity)
- **Improves usability**

### 5. **Structured Pattern** ✅
**Before**: Rectangular grid (6×7 = 42 bricks)
**Now**: Diamond/pyramid (~57 bricks)

**Benefits**:
- More visually interesting
- Traditional Brick Breaker feel
- Better game progression
- Matches reference image
