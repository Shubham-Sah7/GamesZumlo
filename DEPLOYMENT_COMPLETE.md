## Testing the Deployment

### Color Your World
1. Visit: https://app-kappa-two-43.vercel.app/color-your-world
2. You'll see either "Enchanted Garden" or "Cozy Room" (random)
3. Tap any region to color it
4. Use the color wheel to change colors
5. Undo/Clear buttons work
6. Progress milestones show encouraging messages

### Gentle Tap Journey
1. Visit: https://app-kappa-two-43.vercel.app/gentle-tap-journey
2. Tap "Begin Journey" button
3. Follow the 5 tapping rounds:
   - Forehead (3 taps)
   - Cheeks (4 taps)
   - Chest (3 taps)
   - Shoulders (4 taps)
   - Hands (3 taps)
4. Watch for visual feedback (glow + ripple)
5. Complete all rounds to see completion screen
