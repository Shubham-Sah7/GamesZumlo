### What's Needed for Premium Quality

To achieve Headspace/Calm/Lake-level illustration quality, the scenes would need to be:

1. **Hand-drawn by a professional illustrator** using tools like:
   - Adobe Illustrator
   - Procreate
   - Affinity Designer
   - Figma with illustration plugins

2. **Characteristics of premium coloring book art**:
   - Consistent, smooth line weights (1.5-2px)
   - Organic, flowing curves (not geometric shapes)
   - Balanced compositions with clear focal points
   - Rich detail without feeling cluttered
   - Professional proportions and perspective
   - Intentional negative space
   - Visual storytelling

3. **Scene Improvements Needed**:
   - **Enchanted Garden**: More organic flower shapes, flowing stems, natural petal curves, detailed leaves, butterflies with delicate wings, mushrooms with texture
   - **Cozy Room**: Warm furniture, books, plants, windows with curtains, rugs with patterns, cushions, lamps
   - **Underwater World**: Flowing seaweed, coral with organic shapes, fish with natural curves, shells, bubbles, treasure chests, starfish
   - **Moonlight Village**: Houses with character, textured roofs, detailed windows, trees with natural branches, cobblestone paths

### Recommendation

For production-quality illustrations, consider:

1. **Hire a professional illustrator** specializing in:
   - Children's book illustration
   - Coloring book design
   - Mindfulness app illustration
   - Flat illustration style

2. **Reference apps**:
   - Lake (coloring app) - premium line art
   - Headspace - friendly, professional illustrations
   - Calm - beautiful, detailed scenes
   - Colorfy - high-quality coloring pages

3. **Illustration brief**:
   - Style: Friendly, mindful, premium
   - Line weight: Consistent 1.5-2px
   - Complexity: Detailed but not overwhelming
   - Mood: Calming, joyful, inviting
   - Format: SVG paths for web coloring
   - Regions: 40-60 colorable areas per scene
